# Subtle Suits

A duel of hidden armies. Deploy four face-down cards, one per suit, then outplay your rival with suit powers: Spades attack, Hearts steal, Diamonds bomb, Clubs block. Walk your royals home for points; first to 10 wins. Stay subtle: a board totaling 25 or more can be called out.

**Play in your browser:** https://steadystategames.dev/Subtle-Suits/

- Voiced campaign: the Trial of Subtle Suits, 25 opponents across the Four Kingdoms
- Online play with room codes: best of 1, 3 or 5, choose who goes first, rematch, rejoin
- Single player against five bot difficulties, plus a voiced tutorial
